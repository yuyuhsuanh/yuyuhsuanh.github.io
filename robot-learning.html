End to End imitation learning pipeline

The kitchen robot needed to perform complex, contact-rich manipulation tasks that were difficult to hand-code with classical planning. Working with a small team, I was one of the main contributors to building an end-to-end imitation learning pipeline to let the robot learn these behaviors directly from demonstrations. I led the low-latency teleoperation system used to collect expert demonstrations, and worked with teammates on the LeRobot-based training pipeline, which used image and robot-state observations to train a diffusion policy that outputs joint commands. The trained policy was deployed on the real robot and reproduced the demonstrated behaviors successfully in practical tests.
